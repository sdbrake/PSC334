Effects of Exposure

Advanced economies with abundant labor and scarce land: Urban-Rural Conflict
Undeveloped economies with abundant land and scarce labor: Urban-Rural Conflict
Undeveloped economies with abundant labor and scarce land: Class Conflict
Advanced economies with abundant land and scarce labor: Class Conflict

A period of expanding trade

Germany: Capital Poor, Labor Rich, Land Poor.
US: Capital Poor, Labor Poor, Land Rich.
Britain: Capital Rich. Labor Rich. Land Poor.

A period of expanding trade

German Result: Class Conflict. Socialism. Protectionism. Imperialism.
US Result: Urban-Rural Conflict. Populism.
British Result: Expanded franchise. Democratization of institutions.

A period of decreasing trade

In the 1930s, countries began cutting off trade.
Trade exposure affects politics, but politics also affects trade exposure.
