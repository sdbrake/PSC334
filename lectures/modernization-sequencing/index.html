Modernization Sequencing

The Great Divergence

The Great Divergence

  • Some countries successfully become wealthy and stable.
    • Others did not.

Rostow's Modernization Theory:

  • Economic development is a consequence of societal changes.
  • All societies follow a predictable path
  • 5 Stages
    • Traditional society
    • Preconditions for take-off
    • Take-off
    • Drive to maturity
    • Age of high mass consumption

Fukuyama's Political Order Theory:

  • Development is a balance between three forces:
    • Centralized states
    • Rule of law
    • Accountability mechanisms
  • Imbalance = Failure.
  • Wrong Order = Failure.
  • Authoritarianism can promote early development.

Acemoglu and Robinson's Institutionalist Theory:

  • Development is the result of economic activity
    • Economic activity requires stability and property rights.
  • Inclusive institutions lead to protected property rights..
    • Institutions in a feedback loop.
    • Inclusive institutions need to come first.

Potential Sequences

  • Order First: Societies must establish stable political order first, then democracy, then economic growth will follow.
  • Institutions First: Societies must establish inclusive institutions first, then economic growth will follow.
  • Democracy First: Societies must establish rules of the game that lead to health and technology expansion first.
  • Modernization Theory: Societies must have economic transition first, then democracy will inevitably follow.

The problem?

What Comes First?

  • Economy
    • To create social transformation and new needs and desires.
    • To change social norms and weaken tradition.
  • Democracy
    • To create an environment where people can create growth themselves.
    • To reward education and health care.
    • To protect property rights.
  • Bureaucracy
    • To handle the stresses caused by political and economic change that will follow.
    • State capacity is necessary.

Potential Traps

Clientilism

  • Democracy before Bureaucracy.
  • Vote your friends money.
  • Strong institutions blocked.

Bureaucratic Authoritarianism

  • Bureaucracy before Democracy.
  • Block economic changes and political chances that threaten the bureaucracy.

Oligarchy

  • Economy before Democracy.
  • Use economy to enrich leaders, ignore expensive bureaucracy
  • Taxes are a waste of effort.

Fickle Democrats

  • Democracy before Economy.
  • Elected leaders cannot force short term cost for long term gain on the people.
  • Interest groups as veto players
